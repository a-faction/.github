A platform for managing Discord communities with an internal structure, such as roleplay projects, game factions and teams with ranks.

## Why

Large communities usually keep their roster in spreadsheets, hand out warnings verbally and count rewards by hand. History gets lost, decisions are hard to verify and banned people come back on new accounts.

A-Faction keeps all of this in one place. Every action records who did it, when and why.

## Features

- Member roster with history. Members who leave go to an archive
- Warnings and penalties with limits and expiry dates
- Internal currency, rewards and a shop
- Report submission and review
- Interviews with a question bank and voice channel recording
- Blacklists
- Schedule and booking
- Discord bot

Ranks, permissions and penalty rules are configured for each server.

## Where it works

Web dashboard, desktop app for Windows and macOS, Discord bot. Languages: English, Russian, Ukrainian.

## Getting started

Register your server at [a-faction.xyz](https://a-faction.xyz). You need administrator rights on that server.
